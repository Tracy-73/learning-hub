Parent guide & curriculum notes

This unit is designed for a capable Year 7 learner who benefits from short, varied activities. A suggested lesson is 20–30 minutes, but split it when useful. Ask for explanations aloud rather than using worksheets.

Learning sequence: cell structures and functions; plant and animal cells; light microscopy and magnification; specialised cells and adaptations; unicellular organisms; levels of organisation; application and review.

Practical limitation: virtual microscopy helps understanding but does not replace the hands-on KS3 skill of preparing slides and using a real microscope.

How progress works: lesson completion and quiz results are stored in this browser on this device. They do not automatically sync across devices and can disappear if browser website data is cleared.

Privacy: no account, name, email or child information is requested. The page does not send quiz scores to a server. External educational links may have their own privacy policies.

Curriculum reference: England secondary national curriculum and Oak's KS3 Cells unit.
